# Maintainers

People who can accept RFCs and merge to `main`.

Copyright is [eonik](https://www.eonik.ai/)
([github.com/eonik-ai](https://github.com/eonik-ai)). Maintainers steward
the tree; they do not personally own it.

| Name | Role | GitHub |
| --- | --- | --- |
| techievena | project lead | [techievena](https://github.com/techievena) |

To become a maintainer: sustained reviews and at least one accepted RFC
that you did not author. You do not need to work at eonik. The project
lead adds you here in a PR.

Emeritus maintainers stay listed, marked emeritus, and lose merge rights.
